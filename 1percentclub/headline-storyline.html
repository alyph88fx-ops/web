Indicators / 1PC Headline & Storyline

Headline &
Storyline

The official 1 Percent Club indicator for TradingView. The headline is the zone where price should react; the storyline is what price does when it gets there. It marks fresh H4 and H1 supply and demand, support and resistance and breakout lines, and gives buy and sell signals on M1 to M30 with fixed risk.

The headline

Where price should react

Everything is read from closed H4 and H1 candles, so nothing repaints. Boxes are zones; lines are breakouts. Blue means buy, red means sell.

SNR (DBD) · supply

A swing high that price left with drop → base → second drop. Red box from the wick to the candle body. H4 boxes have a solid border, H1 a dashed one.

SNR (RBR) · demand

A swing low that price left with rally → base → second rally. Blue box at the low where the first rally started.

H4 support & resistance

Every H4 swing that price bounced from by at least 1 ATR, drawn from the swing candle's open to its wick. These stay after a test and go when they break.

Breakout lines

When a candle of its timeframe closes through a level, the zone becomes a line at its middle: blue ▲ BUY when broken upward, red ▼ SELL when broken downward.

Forming

A faint dashed box as soon as the first leg and the base are there, before the second leg confirms the DBD or RBR.

Only recent, fresh zones are shown: a zone confirmed in the last 4 trading days that price hasn't tested yet. Support and resistance boxes stay until they break or age out.

The storyline

How a signal forms

A buy needs the bigger picture and the smaller one to agree. A sell is the same, upside down.

  1. 01

    H1 sets the direction

    H1 is bullish after an H1 candle closes above the last H1 swing high, and bearish after one closes below the last H1 swing low.

  2. 02

    M30 breaks out the same way

    An M30 candle closes above the last M30 swing high. The broken level is drawn as a blue line, "M30 breakout ▲ BUY".

  3. 03

    Buy the retest

    A limit order waits at the line and fills when price comes back to it within 16 candles. If price closes back through the line first, the setup is cancelled.

  4. 04

    Fixed risk

    Stop loss 600 points and take profit 1000 points from the entry, drawn as boxes on the chart, with an alert on the order and on the fill.

The indicator also gives a smart zone entry: when price reaches a fresh zone for the first time and rejects it, it places a limit at 50% of the rejection move, inside the zone.

Risk & alerts

The same risk every time

600 / 1000 points

On gold one point is 0.01, so 600 points is $6 and 1000 points is $10, the same as MT5, even on TradingView feeds that quote three decimals. On other symbols one point is the smallest price step (EURUSD: 600 points = 60 pips).

Alerts

  1. On the chart, open Alert and choose 1PC H&S.
  2. Pick Any alert() function call.
  3. You get one message when the limit order is placed and one when it fills.

1PC ENTRY BUY XAUUSD (M30 breakout with the H1 trend) | Entry 3636.00 | SL 3630.00 | TP 3646.00

There are also separate conditions for each event: 1PC Prepare Buy / Sell and 1PC Entry Buy / Sell.

On 60 days of gold M30 data (September to November 2026), buying or selling the retest of an M30 breakout in the H1 direction gave 47 trades with 38% winners: about break-even with 600 / 1000 points. Wider stops at the same ratio did better. Past results don't predict future results.

Install

On your chart in two minutes

Steps

  1. Copy the code below, or download the .pine file.
  2. On tradingview.com, open a chart and then Pine Editor → New → Indicator.
  3. Select everything in the editor, delete it and paste.
  4. Click Save, then Add to chart. Use a 30m or lower chart for signals.

The TradingView phone app has no Pine Editor. On a phone, open tradingview.com in the browser as the desktop site, or install from a computer; the indicator then shows in the app too.

Updating

To move to a newer version, remove 1PC H&S from the chart, paste the new code over the old one in the Pine Editor, save, and add it to the chart again.

Your current version is shown at the top of the code: v1.0 (26 Nov 2026).
