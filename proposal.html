Part 01—The next evolution of the IB industry

The IB industry evolves in cycles. About every three years.

From our research, observations and industry data, we've grouped that evolution into three phases: the past, the present and the future. Each phase is defined by what traders ask for.

T−3 · Signals“Tell me what to trade.”New traders wanted answers.
NOW · Education“Teach me how it works.”Traders want to understand every trade.
T+3 · Automation“Do more of it for me.”Traders will want systems that do the heavy lifting.

02 · Now · Education
03 · T+3 · Automation

Phase 01—The past

SignalsSignals

When retail trading first gained widespread exposure, most people entering the market had little to no trading knowledge. They didn't know where to enter, when to exit or how the market worked. So they asked for signals: someone to tell them what to buy, what to sell and when to execute.

DemandTell me what to trade. Built onThe industry was built around providing answers.
VIP signals · group chatCopy · paste · repeat

Phase 02—The present

EducationEducation

Today the market has matured. Traders are more aware, more selective and want to understand what happens behind every trade. They no longer want to depend entirely on someone else's signals. They want to learn market structure, build strategies, use indicators and become independent traders.

DemandTeach me how it works. Built onThe demand has shifted from signals to education.

Phase 03—The future

AutomationAutomation

Three years ahead, we believe the next major shift is toward automation, artificial intelligence and integrated trading technology. Traders will want systems that analyse, assist, automate and execute with greater efficiency. They won't just want someone to teach them how to trade.

FAQ

Questions about the structure.

What is the blueprint?

A new structure for an IB business: one shared trading ecosystem, with a portfolio of trading brands built on top of it, each with its own community.

Is this an IB?

It works in the IB industry, but it isn't structured like a traditional IB. Growth comes from building brands that give clients a reason to register, a reason to stay and a reason to grow, not from chasing registrations.

What is a portfolio brand?

A trading brand that runs on the blueprint. It has its own identity, voice and community, and it uses the shared ecosystem for education, tools and technology. 88FX and 1 Percent Club are the first two.

Why build for automation now?

We believe the industry moves in cycles of about three years, and the next one is automation, AI and integrated trading technology. Building today puts us among the pioneers of that cycle instead of followers of it.

Can more brands launch on it?

Yes. The structure is built so the next brand launches on the same ecosystem, with every solution ready from day one. Each new brand makes the shared ecosystem worth more to the others.

Where can I see it today?

Both portfolio brands already have first-version sites. 88FX web carries the video library, the ebook shelf and the indicator lab. 1 Percent Club web routes to six rooms, including a trading terminal, a lesson channel and an indicator lab. Each has a member desk that keeps a trader's tools in one place.
